About

Robot Mechanism and System Integration (RMSI) Lab is started on April, 2026 in Fukushima University, Japan

Our lab researches robotics technology including mechanisms and control to solve the practical problem in the world.

Research Highlights

Robotic Mechanisms

We develop and design new mechanisms for robots.

Passive Actuator-Less Grippers

We research about robotic hands using interaction between a hand and an object.

Underwater Robots for Surveying of the Lake

We research an under water robot system that works in the lake.

Deformable Object Manipulation

We research about grasping and handling technology for deformable objects such as fabrics and clothes.

Power-assisted Carts

We research about power-assisted system for supporting carrying heavy objects.
