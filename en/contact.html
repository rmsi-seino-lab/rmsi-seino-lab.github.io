For Undergraduate Students

In the Faculty of Symbiotic Systems Science at Fukushima University, students submit their preferences for graduation research assignments after visiting laboratories during the fifth semester (third year). Students who wish to conduct their graduation research in the RMSI Laboratory (Seino Lab.) should list our laboratory as their first choice.

However, since the number of available positions is limited, if the number of first-choice applicants exceeds the capacity, students will be assigned based on their GPA from the first to the fifth semester, with priority given to students with higher GPAs. Students who wish to join our laboratory are therefore encouraged to work consistently in their regular coursework.

First- and second-year students who are interested in learning more about our lab are welcome to contact us anytime!

For Graduate Students

We welcome applications from students both within Fukushima University and from other universities and Kosen. If you are interested in conducting research in our laboratory, please feel free to contact Prof. Seino to discuss your research interests before applying to the graduate program.

For International Students

We can also accept international students. Please feel free to contact Prof. Seino for more information or to discuss your options.

Lab Tour

If you would like to visit our laboratory, please contact Prof. Seino by email in advance.

For Companies

Our laboratory actively engages in collaborative research with industry. Please see our research seeds on this web page . Companies interested in applying robotics technologies to practical problems or exploring new robot technologies are welcome to contact us by email. You may also contact us through the Industry-Academia Collaboration Office at Fukushima University (Japanese only).
